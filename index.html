      Training and safety guide +

      Progress: When every set reaches the top of its rep range with controlled form, add the smallest practical weight next time.

      Effort: End the set before technique breaks down. The final reps should feel challenging but controlled.

      Coverage: The week covers the major muscle groups for balanced development, including back width, upper-back thickness, lower back and traps.

      Machines first: Alternative lists put stable machine options first. Machines are excellent for growth; use a free-weight option when you also value that specific strength skill or movement challenge.

      Equipment mode: Use the Training equipment selector below the days. The workout, movement image and alternatives update together immediately.

      Alternatives: Use each listed option as a replacement, not an extra exercise. Adding every similar variation creates duplicate volume without adding a new movement pattern.

      Growth and definition: Progressive training and adequate protein build or retain muscle. How visibly “toned” it looks also depends on body-fat level; no single exercise can spot-reduce fat.

      Safety: Stop for sharp or increasing pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, or feeling unwell. Ask a qualified professional when unsure.
